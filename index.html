How to scan with your phone
  1. 1
    Open KIRI Engine (free) or another 3D-scan app and choose Object / Photo scan.
  2. 2
    Sit down and rest your bare foot flat on a plain floor or a sheet of A4 paper. Roll your trousers up above the ankle.
  3. 3
    Slowly walk the phone around the foot at 30–40 cm (2–3 rounds: low, middle, high). Keep the foot still.
  4. 4
    Export the model as STL, OBJ or PLY and upload it here. Then do the same for your left foot.

Ask our staff – we are happy to scan your feet for you in the shop.
